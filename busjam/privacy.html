Privacy Policy

Bus Jam Mosaic Puzzle

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Overview

Bus Jam Mosaic Puzzle is a casual puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not ask you to enter personal information.

Information on your device

Your progress and settings are stored on your device so the game can work. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Information collected when you play

To run and improve the game, the app and the services below may receive information such as app version, device model, operating system version, a randomly generated install identifier, gameplay events, crash reports and diagnostics.

Advertising services may receive your IP address and use it to estimate your general location. The game does not request GPS or precise-location permission.

Where you allow it, the advertising identifier of your device may also be used for advertising and measurement. On iOS this is only after you allow tracking in the system prompt; you can change this at any time in your device settings.

Third-party services

The app uses the following services, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) — usage measurement and crash reports. Privacy
  • AppLovin MAX and Google AdMob — advertising. AppLovin, Google
  • AppsFlyer — install attribution. Privacy
  • Meta — advertising measurement. Privacy

Server-side storage and retention

We store the server-side operational and reporting data we control in Google Firebase. This includes installation records, gameplay events and related app and device information used to operate and improve the game. We retain these records for two months from collection and delete them when the retention period ends.

Google Analytics for Firebase has a two-month retention period for user-level and event-level data. Expired data is removed through Google's regular monthly deletion process. This setting does not apply to standard aggregated reports, which may remain available without individual installation-level records.

Firebase Crashlytics retains crash reports and associated installation identifiers for 90 days before starting to remove them from its live and backup systems, as described in Firebase's privacy information.

Advertising and attribution providers may also process data independently under their own privacy policies. Their retention periods and deletion procedures are described in the linked policies above.

Request deletion of your data

You can request deletion before the retention period ends by emailing protect100for+busjam@gmail.com with the subject Bus Jam Mosaic Puzzle - Data Deletion Request.

Please include the app name, whether you use iOS or Android, your app version and your approximate installation or last-play date. If you know your installation identifier, include it. The game has no account system, so your email address alone may not identify the game's server records. If you do not know your installation identifier, contact us before uninstalling so we can help identify the records.

We may ask for only the additional information needed to locate your installation and verify the request. Once verified, we will delete the associated operational records we control, use the relevant providers' available deletion procedures for data processed on our behalf, and confirm the outcome by email. If a provider processes data independently, we can direct you to its privacy and deletion options. Processing and backup removal follow the relevant provider's deletion procedures.

Do not send passwords, payment information or identity documents with a deletion request.

Uninstalling the app removes its local progress and settings, but does not itself request deletion of server-side data. Changing ad-tracking consent also does not automatically delete previously collected data. See the support page for the deletion request steps.

Your choices

You can limit ad tracking in your device settings. Where required by law, the app asks for your consent before personalized advertising.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Contact

Questions about this policy: protect100for+busjam@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version is always on this page.
