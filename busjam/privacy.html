Privacy Policy

Bus Jam Mosaic Puzzle

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Overview

Bus Jam Mosaic Puzzle is a casual puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not ask you to enter personal information.

Information on your device

Your progress and settings are stored on your device so the game can work. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Information collected when you play

To run and improve the game, the app and the services below may receive information such as app version, device model, operating system version, a randomly generated install identifier, gameplay events, crash reports and diagnostics.

Where you allow it, the advertising identifier of your device may also be used for advertising and measurement. On iOS this is only after you allow tracking in the system prompt; you can change this at any time in your device settings.

Third-party services

The app uses the following services, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) — usage measurement and crash reports. Privacy
  • AppLovin MAX and Google AdMob — advertising. AppLovin, Google
  • AppsFlyer — install attribution. Privacy
  • Meta — advertising measurement. Privacy

Your choices

You can limit ad tracking in your device settings. Where required by law, the app asks for your consent before personalized advertising.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Contact

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version is always on this page.
