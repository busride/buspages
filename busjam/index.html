Bus Jam Mosaic Puzzle

A relaxing color puzzle: send buses out to collect matching beads and reveal the picture underneath.

Each level hides a picture made of colored beads. Tap a bus to send it around the road, where it picks up the beads that match its color. Clear the board to finish the picture and add it to your collection.

There is no timer, so you can take each level at your own pace.
