It helps to include your device model, OS version and the level you were playing.

Request deletion of your data

  1. Email protect100for+busjam@gmail.com with the subject Bus Jam Mosaic Puzzle - Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include the app name, whether you use iOS or Android, your app version and your approximate installation or last-play date. Include your installation identifier if you know it. If you need help identifying it, contact us before uninstalling. Your email address alone may not identify the records because the game has no account system.
  3. We may ask for only the information needed to locate your installation and verify the request. We will delete the associated operational records we control, use providers' available deletion procedures for data processed on our behalf, and confirm the outcome by email. We can also direct you to independent providers' privacy and deletion options.

Do not send passwords, payment information or identity documents.

Operational records are stored in Firebase for two months from collection. Firebase Analytics user-level and event-level data has a two-month retention period and is removed in Google's regular monthly deletion process; standard aggregated reports are not covered by that setting. Firebase Crashlytics retains crash data and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting its deletion process. Provider processing and backup removal follow their respective deletion procedures.

Uninstalling removes local progress and settings; it does not itself delete server-side records. Changing ad-tracking consent does not automatically delete earlier data. Read the privacy policy for the full retention and deletion details.
