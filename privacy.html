Privacy Policy

Bus Ride Sort: Football

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Overview

Bus Ride Sort: Football is designed as a casual puzzle game. We do not require account registration and we do not ask players to provide personal information inside the app.

Information We Collect

The app does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal information from players.

Gameplay progress and settings may be stored locally on your device so the game can function properly.

To operate and improve the app, we may receive device and app diagnostics such as app version, device model, operating system version, crash logs, performance information, installation identifiers, gameplay events, and configuration fetch information.

Firebase and Diagnostics

The app includes Google Firebase services, including Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Remote Config. These services help us understand app stability, diagnose crashes, measure general gameplay usage, and deliver remote configuration values.

Firebase data is processed by Google according to Google's privacy practices. You can learn more at Firebase Privacy and Security and Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising and Tracking

The app does not require account registration and does not sell personal information. If iOS or an integrated SDK requests tracking permission for measurement or attribution, you can allow or deny it through the iOS permission prompt and iOS Settings.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can address the issue.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at wysuperfly@qq.com.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy when the app changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.
