Ads and Rewards

  • Rewarded ad did not grant? Make sure the ad fully completed and the app stayed open until the reward screen returned.
  • No ads available? Ad availability depends on region, network, device settings, and third-party ad fill. Please try again later.
  • Too many ads? Interstitial ads are limited by in-game frequency controls and do not appear during active puzzle moves.

All in-app items are consumable and are granted to your device as soon as the purchase completes, so there is no Restore Purchases flow.

Common Help

  • Restart the app if a level appears stuck.
  • Update to the latest App Store / Google Play version when available.
  • Game progress is stored on your device — deleting the app deletes progress.
  • Check that your device has enough free storage for normal gameplay.
