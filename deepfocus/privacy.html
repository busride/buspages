Privacy Policy

DeepFocus - AI Focus Timer

Last updated: June 16, 2026

Overview

DeepFocus is an offline focus timer app. It does not require account registration and it does not ask you to provide personal information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data.

On-Device Storage

DeepFocus may store focus sessions, task items, timer settings, streaks, soundscape preferences, and app preferences locally on your device using Apple system storage. This information stays on your device and is not accessible to us.

On-Device AI

Smart Task Splitter and focus insights run on-device. The app does not send tasks, focus history, or model data to a server.

Third-Party Services

This launch build does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or external AI services. If future versions add optional purchases through Apple StoreKit, Apple processes purchase transactions under Apple's privacy practices.

Children

DeepFocus is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact protect100for+deepfocus@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy when the app changes. The latest version will be available on this page.
